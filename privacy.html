Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 6Digit Media (“6Digit Media”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit 6digitmedia.com or get in touch with us. 6Digit Media is a digital marketing agency based in Gurugram, Haryana, India.

1. Information we collect

We only collect what we need:

  • Information you give us. When you submit our contact form or email us, we collect your name, email address, and the details of your message.
  • Usage information. If you accept cookies, we collect anonymised analytics data about how you use the site (pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type) to help us improve it.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide the services you request.
  • To understand and improve how our website performs.
  • To comply with our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not send marketing emails unless you have asked us to.

3. Cookies & analytics

Our site uses cookies only after you give consent through our cookie banner. Analytics cookies help us measure traffic and improve the site; your IP address is anonymised. You can decline cookies at any time, and you can clear or block cookies through your browser settings. Declining analytics cookies will not affect your ability to use the site.

4. How we share information

We share your information only with service providers who help us operate the site and respond to you (for example, our email and analytics providers), and only to the extent they need it. We may also disclose information where required by law.

5. Data retention

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and to maintain our business records, after which we delete them.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to how we use it. To make a request, email us at hello@6digitmedia.com.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email hello@6digitmedia.com or write to us at 6Digit Media, Gurugram, Haryana, India.
